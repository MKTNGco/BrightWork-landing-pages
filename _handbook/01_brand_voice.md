## Strict Compliance & Framing
* [cite_start]**Financial Advice:** Ben Olsen is a licensed REALTOR, not a financial advisor or CPA[cite: 959]. [cite_start]Nothing constitutes financial, tax, or legal advice; clients must consult professionals[cite: 960].
* [cite_start]**Capital Programs:** Describe the capability and the benefit[cite: 1248]. [cite_start]Do not specify program names, dollar limits, lender names, or eligibility criteria in marketing copy[cite: 1661].
