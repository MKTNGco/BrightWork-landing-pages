## FAQ

### What is an off-market listing in Lamorinda?

A home sold without a public MLS or portal listing. Sellers often want a quiet sale with no open houses, yard sign, or public days-on-market counter.

### How do I get access to off-market homes before they hit Zillow?

Join a private buyer list maintained by an agent who represents these sellers. The VIP list sends same-day text alerts when new off-market homes arrive.

### Are off-market homes in Moraga and Lafayette available without an agent?

In practice, no. These homes move through agent relationships and private buyer networks, not public portals.

### What areas does the private buyer list cover?

Lamorinda (Moraga, Lafayette, Orinda) and select East Bay pockets.
