On-Market

If the earlier stages don't produce the right result, you launch on the full MLS with the benefit of real feedback from the private and pre-market phases.

Frequently Asked

Quiet listing questions, answered straight.

What is a quiet listing in real estate?

A quiet listing is a home actively marketed to a curated, qualified buyer audience without a public listing on the MLS, the Multiple Listing Service that feeds Zillow, Redfin, and most real estate portals. The sale is structured and compliant with MLS rules, but the home stays off the public market and off the DOM counter, the public counter tracking how long a home has been listed for sale. It is not the same as an informal pocket arrangement. Every quiet listing at BrightWork is documented and run on a clear strategy.

Do quiet listings in Lamorinda sell for less than MLS listings?

Not necessarily. Price is a function of the buyer pool, the home's presentation, and the negotiating dynamic, not the channel alone. Many quiet listings in Lamorinda sell at or near comparable MLS results because the buyer pool is pre-qualified and motivated. If a quiet phase does not produce the right offer, you go to the open market better informed, with no public history of failed days on market.

Is selling a home quietly legal in California?

Yes. Selling a home privately is legal in California when it is done with proper disclosures and consent. Ben uses an MLS-compliant private listing structure with documented seller direction, so the strategy stays within NAR and MLS rules. If you ever want to move from private to public, the path is already mapped out.

How long does a quiet listing phase typically last before going to MLS?

It depends on your goals and the response from the private buyer network. Some sellers run a quiet phase for two to four weeks, then decide whether to extend, shift to a pre-market signal, or go to full MLS. Others sell quietly and never go public. The structure is built around your timeline, not a fixed window.

What is the NAR Clear Cooperation Policy and does it affect quiet listings?

The National Association of Realtors Clear Cooperation Policy requires that a listing be submitted to the MLS within one business day of any public marketing. A properly structured quiet listing respects that rule by avoiding public marketing during the private phase and by documenting seller direction at every step. Ben will walk you through how the policy applies to your specific situation.
