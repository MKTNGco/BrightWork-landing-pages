# BrightFlip

> Pre-sale home improvements funded at attractive terms, managed by Ben, repaid from closing proceeds.

Canonical URL: https://brightflip.brightworkrealty.com/

BrightWork Realty Advocates. Ben Olsen, REALTOR.

## Summary

Pre-sale home improvements funded at attractive terms, managed by Ben, with repayment from closing proceeds when the math favors the seller.

## Audience

Lamorinda (Lafayette, Moraga, and Orinda, California) sellers with deferred maintenance or cosmetic issues who will not fund renovation themselves before listing.

## How it works

- Ben compares realistic as-is sale value to realistic post-improvement value for a defined scope.
- If the math clearly favors the seller, capital and project management proceed with no monthly payments during the project and listing period.
- Repayment comes from closing proceeds. Terms vary by project scope and capital source.
- If the math does not favor the seller, Ben does not recommend the project.


## FAQ

### What home improvements add the most value before selling in Lamorinda?

High-return cosmetic updates that change how buyers feel when they walk in, scoped to what comparable sales support.

### How does pre-sale renovation financing work and who pays for it?

Capital is structured so sellers typically pay nothing out of pocket until close, with repayment from proceeds when a project is approved.

### Who manages the contractors in a BrightFlip project?

Ben's team handles project management, vetted contractors, and timelines aligned to the listing calendar.

### How long does a pre-sale improvement project typically take?

Duration depends on scope. Ben sets expectations before any work begins.
