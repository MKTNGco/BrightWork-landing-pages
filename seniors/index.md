## FAQ

### When should a senior homeowner start thinking about a real estate transition?

Before a crisis forces the decision. Planning ahead preserves more options and autonomy.

### What is Prop 13 and why does it matter if I am thinking about selling?

Long-held California homes often have low assessed values. Selling can trigger capital gains your CPA should model with you first.

### Should I sell my Lamorinda home or keep it as a rental?

Depends on Prop 13 basis, equity, family housing needs, and rental demand. Ben helps evaluate the tradeoffs without giving tax advice.
