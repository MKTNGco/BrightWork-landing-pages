- text: Common Questions
- heading "Off-Market Buying in Lamorinda, Answered" [level=2]
- heading "What is an off-market listing in Lamorinda?" [level=3]
- paragraph: An off-market listing is a home that is being sold without a public listing on the MLS or sites like Zillow and Redfin. In Lamorinda, that usually means a homeowner who wants a quiet sale, no open houses, no yard sign, and no public days-on-market counter. The home is shown selectively to qualified buyers through a private buyer network instead of being broadcast to the whole internet.
- heading "How do I get access to off-market homes before they hit Zillow?" [level=3]
- paragraph: You get access by joining a private buyer list maintained by an agent who actually represents these sellers. Our VIP list is exactly that. When a new off-market home comes to us, you get a text the same day, often before the seller has decided whether to take it public at all. There is no charge to join and your information is never shared with third parties.
- heading "Are off-market homes in Moraga and Lafayette available without an agent?" [level=3]
- paragraph: In practice, no. Off-market homes in Moraga and Lafayette move through agent relationships and private buyer networks, not public portals. If you are not connected to an agent who is in those conversations, you will not see these homes until they hit the MLS, if they ever do. Working with a local REALTOR is how you get a seat at the table.
- heading "What areas does BrightWork's private buyer list cover?" [level=3]
- paragraph: Our private buyer list focuses on Lamorinda, which is Moraga, Lafayette, and Orinda, along with select pockets of the broader East Bay. Ben Olsen and BrightWork Realty Advocates have worked this market since 2004, with team roots in Lamorinda going back to 1977. If you are searching in any of these communities, the list is built for you.
